Neer Investment — Proudly Tanzanian, Committed to Cleanliness

Our Story

From a local vision to a growing Tanzanian brand

Neer Investment was founded with a simple but powerful idea: that every Tanzanian household, business, and institution deserves access to high-quality cleaning and hygiene products at a price that makes sense. What began as a small local operation has grown into a trusted producer of cleaning solutions under the Rabbit Fresh brand.

The Rabbit Fresh name was chosen to represent something fresh, friendly, and dependable qualities that reflect our products and our promise to customers. From our base in Sungwi Street, Temeke, Dar es Salaam, we have built a growing distribution network that reaches retailers, wholesalers, supermarkets, institutions, and businesses across Tanzania.

Today, Rabbit Fresh offers a complete collection of cleaning products Multipurpose Cleaner, Disinfectant, Dishwash, Handwash, and Bar Soap each designed to make everyday cleaning easier, more effective, and more affordable. As we continue to grow, our commitment remains the same: to deliver reliable cleaning and hygiene solutions that Tanzanian families and businesses can count on, every single day.
